  • Confirm the engine is the 2.0L (not the 1.5T) LX trim never came with the turbo, so this should be safe, but check the engine bay sticker or VIN decode to be 100% sure before you commit — you're specifically avoiding the 1.5L turbo for its cold-weather oil dilution issue.
